Privacy Policy

Yeeun Wellness · last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Yeeun Wellness publishing tool (“the tool”) collects when it connects to TikTok, Instagram, Threads or YouTube on behalf of the creator account @yeeun.wellness.

Who this covers

The tool is operated by the creator of the Yeeun Wellness account, for her own account only. It is not a public service and does not accept users.

What we collect

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by the platform you authorise.
  • The account identifier returned by that platform.

We do not collect, store or process: viewer or follower personal data; payment data; location data; or any data belonging to anyone other than the account owner.

Where data is stored

Tokens and the account identifier are stored in a local configuration file on the account owner’s own computer, readable only by that user (file permission 600). No server operated by us receives this data, because we operate no server.

How data is used

Solely to publish content the owner has created and approved, and to read the account’s own posts and replies so the owner can respond.

Third parties

No data is shared, sold, rented or disclosed to any third party. No advertising or analytics provider receives data from this tool.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained until the owner revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform’s own settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security and login → Manage app permissions). Revoking access invalidates the tokens immediately.

Security

Tokens are stored with restricted file permissions, never printed in logs, never transmitted over unencrypted channels, and never shared in messages. Access tokens are short-lived and refreshed automatically.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and processes no data relating to children.

Medical content disclaimer

Content published through the tool is personal experience and general education. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a guarantee of any medical outcome.

Contact

Through direct message at @yeeun.wellness on Instagram or TikTok.
